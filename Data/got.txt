Game of Thrones is a popular fantasy television series that aired on HBO. 

The show is based on the book series A Song of Ice and Fire by George R.R. Martin.

It is set in a fictional world on the continents of Westeros and Essos.Several powerful noble families fight a brutal war to capture the Iron Throne.

Characters face constant danger, political betrayal, and unexpected deaths.

A dark and ancient army of the dead rises in the North to threaten all living people.
