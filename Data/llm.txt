A large language model (LLM) is an advanced artificial intelligence system trained on vast amounts of text to understand and generate human-like language.

It relies on a deep learning framework called a transformer to analyze word contexts and relationships.At its core, it functions as a statistical prediction engine that calculates the most likely next word in a sequence.

Industry overviews like GeeksforGeeks show that popular assistants like ChatGPT and Claude are built on this technology.

Users apply LLMs daily for everyday tasks like translation, coding, text summarization, and interactive chatting.

Chat GPT is also AN LLM.
